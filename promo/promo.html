How should we price the team plan?

There are a few models worth weighing. Seat-based pricing is simple to explain, but it can discourage teams from inviting the people who would get the most value.

Usage-based pricing tends to lower the barrier to adoption, because small teams only pay for what they actually use.

The trade-off is less predictable revenue month to month. A hybrid often works well: a flat platform fee that covers a base level of usage, with metered pricing above it. That keeps the entry point low while giving finance a floor they can plan around.
